# Product specification — short canonical definition

**Business authority:** [MASTER_BLUEPRINT.md](MASTER_BLUEPRINT.md).  
Source: approved Canonical Product Pack (Prompt 0A).

## Product

**Proposal-centered** Proposal, Contract & Procurement Intelligence Platform for L&P Global Security. Tenant-isolated architecture from day one. Optional commercialization does **not** define the core build.

## Primary business outcome

Help L&P build more accurate, competitive, and defensible proposals by learning from verified historical procurement evidence, public buyer/competitor intelligence, pricing history, evaluation results, win/loss outcomes, and current contract/compliance data.

**Proposal work is central.** Contract tracking exists because awarded work, renewals, amendments, pricing, and performance history feed future proposals.

## Primary domain

Government and public-sector procurement are first-class domains, including RFP/RFQ/IFB/task-order/quote workflows, addenda, forms, pricing schedules, evaluations, bid tabs, awards, contract vehicles, wage determinations, certifications, and compliance.

## Core pursuit flow

**Pursuit → Requirements → Pricing → Response → Submission → Result.**

## Full learning loop

Historical Evidence → Pursuit → Requirements → Research/Bid Strategy → Pricing → Response → Submission → Result → Award/Contract → Changes → Renewal/Rebid → verified outcome improves the next pursuit.

## Pursuit workspace

**Overview** — solicitation/buyer/key dates/scope/evaluation; bid strategy; buyer/L&P/competitor intelligence summary; compliance/pricing/response status; next actions/risks.

**Requirements** — source-backed matrix of mandatory/scored/technical/operational/staffing/compliance/legal/pricing/forms/attachments.

**Pricing** — Buyer Requested | L&P Internal Cost | L&P Submitted | Buyer Awarded | Current/Amended. Historical and competitor evidence. **Human final price required.**

**Response** — requirement navigation + Tiptap + Resizable evidence context; approved content; prior outcomes; grounded GPT; L&P Input Required when facts missing.

**Submission** — forms, pricing, references, certifications, insurance, signatures/notarization, addenda, attachments, approvals, outputs, confirmation.

**Result** — pending/won/lost/no-bid/cancelled/no-award; winner; prices; scores/rank; evaluator comments; documented reason; internal lessons; award/contract link.

## Trust pipeline

Source → inventory/checksum/dedupe/version → classify/package → parse/OCR → extract → staging → validation/reconciliation → **human verification** → canonical promotion → eligible search/chunks/embeddings/intelligence.

AI extraction never automatically becomes canonical truth.

## Four commercial truths

1. Buyer Requested — solicitation/addenda/Q&A/requested pricing  
2. L&P Proposed — final submitted proposal/pricing/forms  
3. Buyer Awarded — award notice/PO/executed contract  
4. Current/Amended — executed amendments/modifications/options/renewals  

Never collapse into a generic `rate` or overwrite history.

## Source precedence

- Requirements: latest applicable addendum/official clarification > original solicitation  
- L&P submitted position: final submitted proposal/pricing > draft  
- Current commercial terms: latest executed change > executed contract/PO > award > proposal  

## Proposal evidence states

`VERIFIED_DRAFT_AVAILABLE` | `REVIEW_REQUIRED` | `L&P_INPUT_REQUIRED`

## Content reuse states

`APPROVED` | `REVIEW_REQUIRED` | `DO_NOT_USE` | `SUPERSEDED`

Won does not automatically mean reusable. Lost does not automatically mean worthless.

## AI draft output

`draft_response` · `sources_used` · `assumptions` · `missing_information` · `confidence`

AI must never invent L&P prices, staffing capacity, employee counts, turnover, performance metrics, references, certifications, contract history, capabilities, or margins.

## Past performance integrity

Keep L&P corporate past performance distinct from management prior experience, key-personnel experience, and subcontractor experience.

## Historical Pilot

Start public-first with real L&P procurement evidence. Supplement internal L&P records when available. Use non-L&P security procurement records only as clearly labeled **test corpus** for document/schema coverage.

Target ~20–30 materially different packages/doc sets to prove extraction, schema, provenance, verification, source precedence, and UX before later AI/pricing/response features are treated as complete.
