1\. PRODUCT PURPOSE  
Build one proposal-centered Proposal, Contract & Procurement Intelligence Platform for L\&P Global Security, with multi-tenant-ready architecture from day one.

The platform converts L\&P historical records, current procurement opportunities, public procurement intelligence and awarded contract evidence into:  
VERIFIED \+ SOURCE-BACKED \+ STRUCTURED \+ SEARCHABLE \+ ANALYZABLE \+ REUSABLE INTELLIGENCE.

The primary business objective is not contract storage. It is to help L\&P produce stronger, more accurate and more competitive future responses using evidence from:  
• prior proposals;  
• prior wins and losses;  
• evaluator feedback and scorecards;  
• buyer history;  
• competitor bids/awards/pricing;  
• public procurement records;  
• pricing history and internal costs;  
• contract changes and current rates;  
• compliance/renewal/rebid timing;  
• approved historical proposal content.

3\. PROPOSAL WORK IS CENTRAL  
The platform must support actual proposal production, not merely contract tracking.

For a new solicitation it must help L\&P:  
• understand exactly what the buyer asked;  
• identify deadlines, forms, signatures, attachments and submission method;  
• structure all mandatory/scored requirements;  
• research the buyer and incumbent;  
• analyze prior L\&P history with that buyer/service;  
• analyze relevant competitors, prior awards and evaluation patterns;  
• build an evidence-backed Bid Strategy;  
• determine compliance readiness and missing L\&P information;  
• compare verified historical pricing and current cost/wage evidence;  
• support a human final pricing decision;  
• retrieve approved historical response content;  
• generate grounded requirement-level drafts;  
• expose every source used;  
• flag L\&P INPUT REQUIRED when facts are missing;  
• track proposal/submission completion;  
• produce the working/final proposal package;  
• capture result, evaluator feedback and award;  
• feed what happened back into the intelligence corpus.

• option years, renewals and rebids.

B. Contract & Compliance Intelligence

C. Pricing Intelligence

E. Proposal / Response Intelligence

9\. PURSUIT WORKSPACE  
Tabs:  
Overview | Requirements | Pricing | Response | Submission | Result.

Overview summarizes buyer/solicitation/key dates/scope/evaluation, Bid Strategy, relevant historical intelligence, compliance readiness, pricing/response status and risks.

Requirements is a source-backed matrix of mandatory/scored/technical/operational/staffing/compliance/legal/pricing/forms/attachments requirements.

Pricing is the actual pursuit pricing workbench. It separates Buyer Requested, L\&P Internal Cost, L\&P Submitted, Buyer Awarded, Current/Amended. It shows historical and competitor evidence and requires human final pricing.

Response is the actual proposal authoring workspace. Use requirement navigation \+ Tiptap editor \+ Resizable evidence context. It surfaces approved content, prior wins/losses/evaluator intelligence and source evidence.

Submission validates the full packet: forms, pricing, references, certifications, insurance, signatures/notarization, addenda, attachments, approvals, outputs and confirmation.

Result records pending/won/lost/no-bid/cancelled/no-award, winner, prices, scores/rank, evaluator comments, documented reason, internal lessons and award/contract linkage.

10\. PROPOSAL GROUNDING  
Before generation, each requirement is classified:  
• VERIFIED\_DRAFT\_AVAILABLE  
• REVIEW\_REQUIRED  
• L\&P\_INPUT\_REQUIRED

Historical content reuse states:  
• APPROVED  
• REVIEW\_REQUIRED  
• DO\_NOT\_USE  
• SUPERSEDED

Won ≠ automatically reusable.  
Lost ≠ automatically worthless.  
DO\_NOT\_USE may be used for retrospective analysis but never drafting.

GPT may use only allowed verified/approved evidence and must return:  
• draft\_response  
• sources\_used  
• assumptions  
• missing\_information  
• confidence

When required L\&P facts are not verified, the correct output is L\&P INPUT REQUIRED — not fabrication.

Comparable analysis must show included/excluded records and reasons, descriptive range/statistics, cost floor/threshold, confidence and source evidence.

FINAL PRICE \= HUMAN DECISION REQUIRED.

L\&P PROPOSAL, CONTRACT & PROCUREMENT INTELLIGENCE PLATFORM  
FULL PHASE BUILD PLAN

This is the detailed execution plan. Product maturity follows the original eight-phase business sequence. Legacy engineering/migration IDs may remain in code, but they do not redefine product phase completion.

PHASE 1 — FOUNDATION  
Goal: create the trusted, tenant-safe platform foundation before depending on AI outputs.

Build/validate:  
• Next.js App Router application shell.  
• Supabase Auth, organizations, memberships, tenant-scoped RLS.  
• Supabase Storage evidence vault with append-only/immutable-by-policy handling.  
• Document registry, versions, batches, checksums, processing status.  
• Staging facts, source evidence, verification events, validation exceptions.  
• Vercel Workflow lifecycle abstraction and JobPort.  
• Python/FastAPI/Pydantic processor interfaces.  
• PDF source viewer foundation and verification workspace foundation.  
• Shared schemas/types.  
• Final UX shell: Home, Pursuits, Intelligence, Contracts, Data Ops; Settings separated; Find or Ask GPT in header.

Acceptance:  
• tenant A cannot access tenant B data/files;  
• evidence is never casually overwritten;  
• AI-extracted facts default to unverified;  
• source/page/cell provenance can be retained;  
• lint/typecheck/build pass;  
• no fake production data is required to make the UI look complete.

Out of scope:  
• autonomous pricing;  
• proposal drafting as production capability;  
• unsupported analytics;  
• commercial billing.

PHASE 2 — REAL-DOCUMENT HISTORICAL PILOT  
Goal: prove the data model and document workflow against real procurement evidence before designing the rest from assumptions.

Corpus strategy:  
• begin with verified public L\&P procurement records and actual L\&P-originated proposals/contracts/POs/evaluations where obtainable;  
• supplement with real security-industry solicitations/forms/workbooks only as TEST CORPUS when an L\&P-specific document type is unavailable;  
• never label another contractor’s content as L\&P history;  
• target approximately 20–30 materially different procurement packages/doc sets selected for coverage, not random mentions.

First-wave package types should force the system to understand:  
• solicitation/RFP/RFQ/IFB;  
• addenda/Q\&A;  
• L\&P proposal/response narrative;  
• required forms/signatures;  
• pricing schedule/workbook;  
• staffing/sites/service plan;  
• evaluation scorecard/bid tab;  
• win/loss/award;  
• executed contract;  
• PO;  
• amendment/change/option/renewal;  
• compliance evidence.

Process every pilot document through:  
Intake → checksum/version → package association → parse/OCR → extraction → staging → validation/reconciliation → human verification → canonical promotion.

Measure:  
• extraction accuracy;  
• requirement recall;  
• pricing-cell accuracy;  
• entity/date accuracy;  
• forms/checkbox/table capture;  
• page/sheet/cell provenance;  
• version/addendum handling;  
• source precedence;  
• verification usability;  
• processing time/cost.

Required deliverable:  
PILOT\_GAP\_REPORT showing, for each package:  
• what captured correctly;  
• missing schema/entity/field;  
• extraction/parser failure;  
• provenance/verification problem;  
• source-precedence issue;  
• required schema change;  
• UX placement;  
• whether the issue is blocking or deferrable.

Acceptance:  
• real documents can be reviewed against source;  
• at least representative package types complete the trust pipeline;  
• gaps are evidence-backed, not theoretical;  
• no later AI feature is declared complete because a placeholder page exists.

PHASE 3 — PRODUCTION HISTORICAL INGESTION & MIGRATION

Build/finish:

PHASE 6 — SEARCH, ASK GPT, AI REPORTS & AUTOMATION  
Goal: make the verified corpus usable conversationally and proactively without turning the product into a generic chatbot.

First-class global capability:  
Find or Ask GPT in the authenticated header.

Modes:  
LOCATE — structured SQL/FTS/direct retrieval, no LLM required.  
ASK/ANALYZE — structured \+ FTS \+ pgvector \+ verified evidence \+ GPT synthesis.  
REPORT — supported intelligence report generation.

Purpose-aware retrieval must enforce:  
• tenant/org;  
• permissions;  
• verification;  
• current/historical version;  
• source precedence;  
• outcome;  
• reuse status;  
• retrieval purpose.

Purposes include:  
GENERAL\_QA, LOCATE, LOSS\_ANALYSIS, COMPETITOR\_ANALYSIS, PRICING\_ANALYSIS, BID\_STRATEGY, PROPOSAL\_DRAFTING, COMPLIANCE\_REVIEW, REPORT\_GENERATION.

AI answer contract:  
• Answer;  
• Sources/Evidence;  
• Data scope;  
• limitations/confidence where relevant;  
• View Source.

If evidence is insufficient, say so explicitly.

Reports:  
• Bid Strategy;  
• Buyer Intelligence Brief;  
• Market Intelligence;  
• Competitor Intelligence;  
• Pricing Intelligence;  
• Win/Loss Analysis;  
• Proposal Improvement/Evaluator Analysis;  
• Executive Intelligence Brief.

Automation is first-class but bounded:  
• renewal/compliance reminders;  
• document-processing workflows;  
• missing/failed verification alerts;  
• scheduled intelligence/report refresh where justified;  
• optional future research-monitoring jobs;  
• human approval gates for consequential actions;  
• never autonomously set final bid price, make unsupported claims, or submit a proposal without explicit workflow approval.

Acceptance:  
• LOCATE and ASK are distinct;  
• answers cite source evidence;  
• blocked content cannot leak into proposal drafting;  
• reports disclose scope/limitations;  
• automation does not bypass verification/approval.
